Capture what matters, instantly
Photo or voice — BaceTech turns a 30-minute paper report into a 60-second AI-analyzed incident record. Description, probable cause, recommended solution, and applicable norms — all generated automatically.
Build institutional memory
Every incident captured becomes searchable knowledge. After 90 days, BaceTech identifies patterns humans miss — recurring categories, high-risk trades, projects that need attention. Your next project starts smarter than your last.
Stay compliant, stay protected
Every record is timestamped, geotagged, and audit-ready. BaceTech automatically references applicable norms (ABNT NBR, NRs, DTU) — so when inspectors or lawyers come knocking, your documentation speaks for itself.
